Use when services with external dependencies (payments gateway, notification
providers) degrade and the WAN link shows latency or loss.

Read-only diagnostic steps:
1. Show latency, jitter and loss on the WAN interface.
2. Compare with carrier-side probe results.
3. Check wan-1 CPU and interface errors.
4. Escalate to network on-call to open a carrier ticket.
