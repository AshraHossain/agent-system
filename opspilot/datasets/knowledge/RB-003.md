title: Packet loss on leaf switches (legacy procedure)
status: deprecated
updated: 2021-04-07

components: [leaf]
tags: [packet loss, leaf, reboot, errors]
---
If a leaf switch shows packet loss on an uplink, reboot the leaf switch and
clear the interface counters. If loss persists after reload, shut down the
affected uplink so traffic moves to the other spine.
