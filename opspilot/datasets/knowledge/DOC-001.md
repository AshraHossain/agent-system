Each leaf has two 40G uplinks, one to each spine, load-balanced with ECMP.
Loss or congestion on one uplink affects roughly half of the flows leaving
that leaf, producing intermittent rather than total packet loss. Spines
connect to their own core router; the firewall is dual-homed to both cores.
