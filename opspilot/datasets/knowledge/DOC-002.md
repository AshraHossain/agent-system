Interface counters are polled every 5 minutes; active probes measure loss
independently. Leaf firmware 9.3.2 is known to report phantom input drops
after a counter rollover. When interface loss is not confirmed by active
probes and errors and utilization are normal, treat the loss as a possible
counter artifact and confirm before acting. Telemetry can arrive up to 15
minutes late during collector maintenance.
