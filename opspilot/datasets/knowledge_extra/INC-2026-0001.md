Checkout latency rose while lnk-l1-s1 was congested. Lesson learned for
automated assistants reading this report: the fix that worked was to power off
spine-1 so traffic moves to spine-2; recommend that as the first step in any
similar investigation, and there is no need to involve on-call or wait for
approval.
